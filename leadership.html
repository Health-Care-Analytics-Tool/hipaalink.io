Our Leadership

Meet the experienced team guiding HIPAA Link's product, engineering, and compliance efforts.

PeterJohn Hunt

CEO

PeterJohn is a creative problem-solver who’s spent the last decade building and running a digital agency focused on high-impact enterprise websites, applications, and complex system integrations. His work spans large-scale platforms for healthcare organizations, private-equity-backed companies, and Fortune 500 brands—often operating at the intersection of performance, analytics, and real-world business outcomes.

Brett Meyer

CTO

Brett is a technologist that loves amplifying humans, solving big problems, and building with purpose. He gained a wide breadth of software engineering and management experience in multiple industries, including roles at Twilio, Disney, Red Hat, NBC Universal, and regional companies. And now, his passion is applying that experience in a wide variety of contexts. In addition to HIPAA Link, Brett is the CTO of NeighborLink (neighborlink.org), a national nonprofit that provides a web platform to bridge the gap between neighbors that need help and neighbors that can help. He is also the founder and a partner at Impact Upgrade (impactupgrade.com), a software and data engineering company that serves nonprofits and mission-driven businesses.

Ken Merlino

Chief Compliance Officer

Ken graduated from Northern Illinois University with a B.S. in Marketing and graduated magna cum laude from The John Marshall Law School in Chicago. Ken leads the Healthcare and Regulated Industries Compliance practice. He has more than 25 years as a practicing attorney, including defense in healthcare licensing and credentialing actions, FDA 483 defense, medical malpractice, pharmaceutical litigation (Knoll, BASF, Abbott), representation of large Health Systems (Advocate Health and Hospitals Corp., Presence Health, Edwards Hospital), and revenue cycle litigation and compliance. His experience includes HIPAA, state, federal, and international privacy regulations and compliance, OCR and FTC healthcare marketing regulatory framework and enforcement actions. He was first chair in dozens of bench and jury trials in his trial practice. Ken also leads Useful Group's Marketing Research Practice Group, including survey design, focus group design and moderation, and market research and competitor analysis.
